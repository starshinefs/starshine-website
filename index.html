FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need to provide cleaning products or equipment?

No. Our team brings professional equipment and eco-friendly products. If you'd prefer we use your own, just let us know.

Are your cleaners insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Every cleaner is DBS checked and vetted, and we are fully insured for your peace of mind.

Can I book a one-off clean?

Absolutely. We offer one-off, weekly, fortnightly and monthly cleans for homes and businesses.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back to put it right.
